Subrata Pal

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Chemistry at Oklahoma State University, working with Professor Spencer P. Pitre on cobalt-photocatalyzed reactions — studying first-row transition metal (Co) photocatalysts and their use in carbon–carbon bond-forming reactions for pharmaceutically relevant molecules. Currently seeking a postdoctoral researcher position in photoredox catalysis or synthetic methodology development, with long-term interests in an independent academic research career.
